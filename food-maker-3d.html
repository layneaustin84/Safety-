How the food travels

The numbers show the order, from the top of the machine to the plate.

  1. 1
    Ingredient hoppersFlour, milk and eggs wait in three funnels.
  2. 2
    Mixing bowlA spinning paddle stirs everything into batter.
  3. 3
    Hot griddleBatter pours out and cooks. Orange glow = hot!
  4. 4
    Flipper armA robot spatula flips the pancake over.
  5. 5
    Conveyor beltCarries the finished pancake to the plate.
  6. 6
    Control panelThe computer "brain" that runs each step.
Height: about 1 meter (as tall as a 4th grader's shoulder)
Power: plugs into a wall outlet, like a toaster
Pancakes per minute: 2
Safety: hot parts are behind the clear window; the red button stops everything
